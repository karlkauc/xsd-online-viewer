ISO 20022 message definitions are published as XML Schemas in the
[ISO 20022 message catalogue](https://www.iso20022.org/catalogue-messages). They are large, deeply nested and
reuse the same building blocks under different names — exactly what a diagram helps with.

| Message | Name | Root element |
|---|---|---|
| [pain.001](/standards/iso-20022/pain-001) · [open pain.001.001.12](/iso20022?message=pain.001.001.12) | Customer Credit Transfer Initiation | `CstmrCdtTrfInitn` |
| [pain.002](/standards/iso-20022/pain-002) · [open pain.002.001.14](/iso20022?message=pain.002.001.14) | Customer Payment Status Report | `CstmrPmtStsRpt` |
| [camt.053](/standards/iso-20022/camt-053) · [open camt.053.001.12](/iso20022?message=camt.053.001.12) | Bank To Customer Statement | `BkToCstmrStmt` |
| [camt.054](/standards/iso-20022/camt-054) · [open camt.054.001.12](/iso20022?message=camt.054.001.12) | Bank To Customer Debit Credit Notification | `BkToCstmrDbtCdtNtfctn` |
| [pacs.008](/standards/iso-20022/pacs-008) · [open pacs.008.001.13](/iso20022?message=pacs.008.001.13) | FI To FI Customer Credit Transfer | `FIToFICstmrCdtTrf` |

The [ISO 20022 tab](/iso20022) lists more than 300 messages — payments initiation, clearing and settlement, cash
management, collateral, remittance — in every version, searchable by code, name or root element, and loads any of
them with one click. They come unchanged from a public mirror of the official
[ISO 20022 message catalogue](https://www.iso20022.org/catalogue-messages).
