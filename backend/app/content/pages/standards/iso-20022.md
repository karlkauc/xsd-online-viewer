ISO 20022 message definitions are published as XML Schemas in the
[ISO 20022 message catalogue](https://www.iso20022.org/catalogue-messages). They are large, deeply nested and
reuse the same building blocks under different names — exactly what a diagram helps with.

| Message | Name | Root element |
|---|---|---|
| [pain.001](/standards/iso-20022/pain-001) | Customer Credit Transfer Initiation | `CstmrCdtTrfInitn` |
| [pain.002](/standards/iso-20022/pain-002) | Customer Payment Status Report | `CstmrPmtStsRpt` |
| [camt.053](/standards/iso-20022/camt-053) | Bank To Customer Statement | `BkToCstmrStmt` |
| [camt.054](/standards/iso-20022/camt-054) | Bank To Customer Debit Credit Notification | `BkToCstmrDbtCdtNtfctn` |
| [pacs.008](/standards/iso-20022/pacs-008) | FI To FI Customer Credit Transfer | `FIToFICstmrCdtTrf` |

Load the XSD of the version you need by upload or URL; the viewer works with any ISO 20022 message the same way.
