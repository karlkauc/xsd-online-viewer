FundsXML 4.2.6 was published on 2024-05-06 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/4.2.6) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd` (+ `xmldsig-core-schema.xsd`)
- **Target namespace:** none
- **Root element:** `FundsXML4`
- **Complex types:** 244, **simple types:** 54, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 4.2.5

**New types:** `AddressTypeDataDEType`, `AnnualReturnsDataDEType`, `LatitudeDataDEType`, `LongitudeDataDEType`, `PurchasesDataDEType`, `SalesDataDEType`, `UsageDataDEType`

### Changed content models

- `CountrySpecificFundDataDEType`
  - added `RealEstateReporting (inline type, 0..1)`
  - removed `(base) (extension of anyType)`
- `TransactionType`
  - added `ExecutionTimestamp (dateTime, 0..1)`
  - added `Exchange (MICCodeType, 0..1)`
