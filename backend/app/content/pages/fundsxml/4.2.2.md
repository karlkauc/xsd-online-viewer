FundsXML 4.2.2 was published on 2023-03-07 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/4.2.2) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd` (+ `xmldsig-core-schema.xsd`)
- **Target namespace:** none

- **Complex types:** 232, **simple types:** 51, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 4.2.1
