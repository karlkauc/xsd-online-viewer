FundsXML 3.2.0 was published on 2020-03-25 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/3.2.0) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd`
- **Target namespace:** `http://www.fundsxml.org/XMLSchema/3.2`
- **Root element:** `FundsXML`
- **Complex types:** 125, **simple types:** 44, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 3.1.1

**New types:** `AssetMasterCountryDEType`, `CICCodeType`, `CQSType`, `CodificationType`, `ContributionToSCRType`, `CreditRiskDataType`, `EconomicArea2Type`, `EconomicAreaType`, `IssuerType`, `NACECodeType`, `OwnerIdentifierDEType`, `RedemptionType`, `SIICouponFrequencyType`, `SIIIssuerCodificationType`, `SIIPositionType`, `SIISecurityCodificationType`, `TripartiteTemplateSolvencyIIType`, `VendorAdditionalDataDEType`, `YesNoL1Type`

**Removed types:** `QualificationType`, `ReportingDE_BuBaType`, `ReportingDE_VAGType`, `VoeigKategorisierungsType`
