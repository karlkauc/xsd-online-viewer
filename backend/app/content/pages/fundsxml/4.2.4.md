FundsXML 4.2.4 was published on 2024-01-04 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/4.2.4) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd` (+ `xmldsig-core-schema.xsd`)
- **Target namespace:** none
- **Root element:** `FundsXML4`
- **Complex types:** 235, **simple types:** 52, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 4.2.3

### Changed content models

- `FundStaticDataType`
  - added `OngoingCosts (inline type, 0..1)`
  - added `SFDRProductType (inline type, 0..1)`
- `TransactionType`
  - added `OrderTimestamp (dateTime, 0..1)`
  - added `OrderPrice (AmountType, 0..1)`
