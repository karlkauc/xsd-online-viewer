FundsXML 4.2.10 was published on 2026-01-23 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/4.2.10) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd` (+ `xmldsig-core-schema.xsd`)
- **Target namespace:** none
- **Root element:** `FundsXML4`
- **Complex types:** 267, **simple types:** 52, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 4.2.9

**New types:** `EMTType_V43`, `EMT_V43_FinancialInstrumentType`

**Removed types:** `AUMType`, `BenchmarkType`, `CodificationType_V6`, `CodificationType_V7`, `ContributionToSCRType_V5`, `ContributionToSCRType_V6`, `ContributionToSCRType_V7`, `CounterpartyType`, `CountrySpecificEMT41DataType`, `CreditRiskDataType_V5`, `CreditRiskDataType_V6`, `EETYesNoNeutralType`, `PercentageStrategyCoverageType1.1.2`, `PositionValuationType`, `RedemptionType_V5`, `RedemptionType_V6`, `SIICouponFrequencyType_V5`, `SIICouponFrequencyType_V6`, `SIIPositionType_V5`, `Text2Type`, `Text300Type`, `Text8Type`

### Changed content models

- `AccountType`
  - added `AccountType (inline type, 0..1)`
- `CountrySpecificFundDataATType/OeNB`
  - removed `FeesPaidByShareholders (AmountType, 0..1)`
- `DirectReportingType`
  - added `EMT_V4_3 (EMTType_V43, 0..1)`
- `TransactionType`
  - changed `Expenses: FundAmountType, 0..1 → inline type, 0..1`
