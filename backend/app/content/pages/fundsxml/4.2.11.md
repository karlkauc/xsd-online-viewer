## Release notes

#### What's Changed

Notable additions since 4.2.9
-----------------------------
* EMT V4.3: new MiFID II EMT template version (EMT_V4_3, EMT_V43_*,
  EMTType_V43) including VCA reporting fields.
* Derivatives & transactions: dedicated Future / Option / Swap transaction
  structures, option characteristics (exercise date/quantity, premium,
  contracts), day-count convention, reset/term details, UTI references.
* Central clearing & collateral: CCP / clearing status, tri-party agent,
  collateral baskets and items (nominal, market value), variation margin,
  rehypothecation flag, repo rate, general collateral flag.
* Identifiers: additional instrument identifiers (FIGI, CFI).
* Country / regulatory: French additional information and VCA methodology
  fields; UK and UCITS ongoing-cost fields.

* feat(repo): comprehensive repo refactoring for TransactionData and AssetMasterData by @karlkauc in https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/pull/11
