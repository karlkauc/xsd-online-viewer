FundsXML 4.1.6 was published on 2020-03-25 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/4.1.6) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd`
- **Target namespace:** none
- **Root element:** `FundsXML4`
- **Complex types:** 188, **simple types:** 43, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 4.1.5

**New types:** `CodificationType_V5`, `ContributionToSCRType_V5`, `CreditRiskDataType_V5`, `EMTType_V3`, `EMTYesNeutralType`, `EMT_V3_FinancialInstrumentType`, `RedemptionType_V5`, `SIICouponFrequencyType_V5`, `SIIIssuerType_V5`, `SIIPositionType_V5`, `SIISecurityCodificationType_V5`, `SolvencyIIPortfolioType_V5`, `TripartiteTemplateSolvencyIIType_V5`

### Changed content models

- `CreditRiskDataType`
  - changed `InstrumentIssuer: SIIIssuerType, 1..1 → SIIIssuerType_V5, 1..1`
  - changed `IssuerGroup: SIIIssuerType, 1..1 → SIIIssuerType_V5, 1..1`
- `DirectReportingType`
  - added `EMT_V3 (EMTType_V3, 0..1)`
- `IndirectReportingType`
  - added `TripartiteTemplateSolvencyII_V5 (TripartiteTemplateSolvencyIIType_V5, 0..1)`
