FundsXML 4.2.0 was published on 2022-05-04 in the [fundsxml/schema](https://github.com/fundsxml/schema/releases/tag/4.2.0) repository.

- **Main schema:** `FundsXML.xsd`
- **Target namespace:** none
- **Root element:** `FundsXML4`
- **Complex types:** 217, **simple types:** 50, **global elements:** 1

## Changes since FundsXML 4.1.10

**New types:** `CodificationType_V6`, `ContributionToSCRType_V6`, `CreditRiskDataType_V6`, `EETEnhancedExclusionType`, `EETExclusionType`, `EETReportType`, `EETWeightNumberType`, `EETYesNeutralType`, `EETYesNoNeutralType`, `EETYesNoType`, `EETsType`, `EMTType_V4`, `EMT_V4_FinancialInstrumentType`, `PRIIPSType_V20`, `PRIIPS_V2_Country_Specific_DE`, `PRIIPS_V2_NarrativesType`, `PRIIPS_V2_ReturnScenarioType`, `PercentageStrategyCoverageType`, `RedemptionType_V6`, `SIICouponFrequencyType_V6`, `SIIIssuerType_V6`, `SIIPositionType_V6`, `SIISecurityCodificationType_V6`, `SolvencyIIPortfolioType_V6`, `TripartiteTemplateSolvencyIIType_V6`, `ValueStrategyCoverageType`

### Changed content models

- `DirectReportingType`
  - added `EMT_V4 (EMTType_V4, 0..1)`
  - added `EETs (EETsType, 0..1)`
- `IndirectReportingType`
  - added `TripartiteTemplateSolvencyII_V6 (TripartiteTemplateSolvencyIIType_V6, 0..1)`
  - added `EPT_V20 (PRIIPSType_V20, 0..1)`
